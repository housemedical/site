Your safety

Home safety

Plan escape routes, test smoke alarms monthly, and keep cooking areas clear. Request a free Home Fire Safety visit.

Request a visit

Road safety

Slow down, wear a seatbelt, and never drive under the influence. Keep a breakdown kit in your car.

Outdoor safety

Avoid disposable BBQs on dry grass, and fully extinguish campfires. Pack water and check local restrictions.

Your legal duties

Businesses must carry out a suitable fire risk assessment, maintain fire detection and alarm systems, provide staff training, and keep escape routes clear.
