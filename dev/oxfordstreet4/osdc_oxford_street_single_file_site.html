Travel & Getting Around

Oxford Street is served by multiple Underground lines and bus routes, with step‑free access at several stations. Plan ahead and check live service updates on the day of travel.

  • Underground Nearby stations: Oxford Circus (Bakerloo/Central/Victoria), Bond Street (Central/Elizabeth), Tottenham Court Road (Central/Elizabeth), Marble Arch (Central).
  • Bus Frequent routes along and around Oxford Street throughout the day and night. Use TfL for the latest information.
  • Cycle Cycle parking on side streets; Santander Cycles docking nearby. Please ride with care—busy pedestrian areas.
  • Accessibility Step‑free access at Bond Street and Tottenham Court Road (Elizabeth line). Many stores offer assisted entry—contact in advance.
  • From the Airports Elizabeth line connects directly from Heathrow to Bond Street & Tottenham Court Road.

About OSDC

Oxford Street Development Corporation (OSDC) champions the evolution of Oxford Street as a world‑class destination — celebrating heritage while welcoming innovation. We convene retailers, cultural partners, landlords and the local community to create a street that is vibrant, safe and inclusive.

Our work spans public realm improvements, programming and placemaking, business engagement, and strategic partnerships that support a thriving West End.
