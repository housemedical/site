Our Mission & Values

More Than Just a Fire Service

We are committed to helping you become safer, healthier, and to live more independently. Our work focuses on prevention, protection, and resilience, ensuring our communities in Dorset and Wiltshire are as safe as possible.

  • Protecting you and the built environment from harm.
  • Providing a professional and prompt emergency response.
  • Supporting and developing our dedicated staff.
