Accessibility Information

We're committed to making Oxford Street accessible to everyone

Mobility Access

  • Step-free access at major tube stations
  • Accessible entrances to most large stores
  • Dropped kerbs at all pedestrian crossings
  • Blue badge parking spaces available
  • Wheelchair-accessible toilets in major stores

Visual Accessibility

  • Audio announcements at tube stations
  • Tactile paving at crossing points
  • Large print maps available
  • Guide dog friendly environment
  • Audio description services in some venues

Hearing Accessibility

  • Hearing loops in customer service areas
  • Visual announcements on transport
  • Sign language interpreters available (advance booking)
  • Text-based information services
  • Quiet zones in major department stores

Support Services

  • Personal shopping assistance
  • Accessibility guides available
  • Rest areas and seating throughout
  • Accessible changing facilities
  • Support for hidden disabilities
