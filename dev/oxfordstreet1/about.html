About OSDC

Leading the transformation of Oxford Street while preserving its heritage and enhancing its position as London's premier shopping destination.

Our Mission

The Oxford Street Development Corporation is dedicated to transforming Oxford Street into a world-class destination that celebrates London's retail heritage while embracing innovation, sustainability, and inclusive growth.

Heritage Preservation

Protecting and restoring Oxford Street's architectural heritage while adapting historic buildings for modern use.

Sustainability

Leading London's green revolution with innovative environmental initiatives and carbon-neutral development.

Community Focus

Creating inclusive spaces that serve Londoners, visitors, and businesses while supporting local communities.

Shaping London's premier shopping destination with heritage and luxury at its heart.
