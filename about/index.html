Our approach

Start with the problem.
Stay with the detail.

We believe useful software comes from understanding both the people who use it and the work it needs to do.

The studio

We make complicated
things feel straightforward.

House Medical builds software for business and public-service problems. Our focus is on practical products that give people a clearer way to complete an important task.

That takes thoughtful design and careful engineering. We treat them as parts of the same job.

01

Understand.

Start with the task, not the screen.

Work out what people are trying to do, what information matters and where the process becomes difficult.

02

Simplify.

Make the next step feel obvious.

Turn that understanding into a clear journey. Use plain language, ask for what is needed and make important decisions visible.

03

Build.

Care about what happens underneath.

Bring the interface, business rules and integrations together. Pay attention to validation, errors and the less obvious cases.

04

Refine.

Keep learning from real use.

Look at where people need help, where they hesitate and what could work better. Improve the details that make a difference.
