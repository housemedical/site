House Medical Environmental & Sustainability Policy

At House Medical, we are committed to environmental sustainability. Key aspects of our Environmental & Sustainability Policy include:


  • Net-Zero Ambition: We are committed to achieving net-zero carbon emissions by 2030 and continually reducing our carbon footprint to meet this goal.
  • Sustainable Procurement: We try to embed sustainable procurement practices across our healthcare supply chains by selecting products and services that minimize environmental impact.
  • Low-Impact Operations: We are committed to low-impact office operations and digital-first working practices, reducing waste and conserving energy wherever possible.
  • Reduced Travel Emissions: We try to minimize travel-related emissions by prioritizing virtual engagements and using low-emission transport options when travel is necessary.
  • Responsible Partnerships: We are committed to working with environmentally responsible partners and suppliers to encourage sustainable practices throughout our network.

Each of these commitments reflects our professional and aspirational approach to sustainability. We aim to act responsibly and transparently, continually improving our practices to support a healthier planet for future generations.
