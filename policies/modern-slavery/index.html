Modern Slavery Statement

Our commitmentsView all policiesContact us

House Medical has a zero-tolerance approach to modern slavery, forced labor, and human trafficking. We are committed to acting ethically and with integrity in all our business dealings. Any form of exploitation is unacceptable in our operations or supply chains.

We try to prevent modern slavery in every aspect of our business. This means being vigilant in our procurement and partnership practices. We work diligently to identify and address any signs of forced labor, and we will take appropriate action if any concerns arise. We will not knowingly support or do business with any organization involved in forced labor, human trafficking, or other forms of modern slavery.

Our commitment is broad and applies to all suppliers and partners, regardless of sector or geography. House Medical operates across various industries (not just healthcare), and we hold the same high ethical standards everywhere we work. We expect our suppliers and business partners to share these values and uphold fair, humane labor practices in their own operations.

This Modern Slavery Statement reflects our unwavering stance on human rights and fair labor. We remain vigilant and will continue to promote ethical standards throughout our business, fostering a supply chain that respects the dignity and rights of all individuals.
