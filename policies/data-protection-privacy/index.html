A note on privacy.

How we handle information shared with us through this website.

Questions?sales@housemedical.co.uk

This website

This website introduces our software work. It does not have an enquiry form, advertising trackers or third-party analytics scripts. Fonts and visual assets are served with the site. If you change the motion setting, that preference is saved in your browser on this device.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, the information in your message and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond and manage the conversation. Please only share information that is relevant to your enquiry.

Service providers

Our hosting and email providers process information needed to operate their services. This may include technical information about requests to the website and email delivery.

Our software products

Information about how a particular product handles data should be read alongside that product’s own privacy information and terms.

Your information

To ask about personal information you have shared with us, including a request to access, correct or delete it, email sales@housemedical.co.uk.
