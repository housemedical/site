Health & Safety Policy

Our commitmentsView all policiesContact us

At House Medical, we are committed to maintaining the highest standards of health and safety across our organisation. We try to foster a proactive culture that prioritises well-being, both in our own workplaces and in the environments we share with our clients.

Our Commitments

Safe Work Environment

We provide a safe and healthy workplace for our team, whether they are in the office, working remotely, or on a client site. By identifying and addressing potential hazards early, we strive to prevent accidents and protect everyone's physical health.

Mental Health & Well-being

We recognise the importance of mental health and offer support and resources to our employees. In particular, we try to ensure remote team members are not isolated by regularly checking in on their well-being and encouraging a healthy work-life balance.

Proactive Approach

Our approach to health and safety is proactive rather than reactive. We encourage our team to speak up about any concerns and continuously seek ways to improve our practices, learning from experience and industry best practice.

Clients & Partners

We prefer to work with clients and partners who also engage seriously with health and safety practices. By collaborating with like-minded organisations, we uphold strong health and safety standards on every project we undertake.

Through these commitments, House Medical maintains a workplace where health and safety are at the forefront, reflecting our duty of care as healthcare professionals and our dedication to the well-being of all those we work with.
